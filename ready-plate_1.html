Restart Edge to play

A restart may bring Edge's graphics back.

  1. 1Press Copy address.
  2. 2Open a new tab.
  3. 3Paste it at the top. Press Enter.
  4. 4Edge restarts. The game opens again.

If it still does not start, update this computer's graphics driver.

edge://restart
